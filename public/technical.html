how seedless is built

seedless is a money app for people who get paid in dollars. under the hood it runs on solana. this page is the short technical version: what signs, who pays the fees, and what we run ourselves.

sign-in: a passkey, no seed phrase

every account is a smart account on solana, built on lazorkit. the key that controls it is a passkey on your phone, unlocked with face ID or fingerprint. there is no seed phrase to write down and no password to leak.

seedless is non-custodial. your passkey signs every move of your money. we never hold your funds and we can't move them for you.

session signing

for quick usdc sends the app can open a session, so you don't face-scan for every tap. a session lasts about a day and can only send usdc, up to a cap per send and per session. those rules are enforced on chain by the lazorkit program, so they hold even if the session key is taken from the phone. anything else, payouts included, asks for your passkey.

network fees: we pay them

you never need sol to use seedless. our own paymaster signs as the fee payer and covers the network fee. it runs in our api and pays from a dedicated gas pool, with guardrails before it signs anything:

  • it only signs transactions whose instructions are lazorkit account instructions, so no other program can be handed the fee payer
  • priority fees are capped, so a transaction can't ask the pool for an outsized fee
  • every transaction is simulated first, and it is refused if the pool would lose more than the rent and fee of a new account
  • the pool balance is watched, and the team gets an alert with the address before it runs low

payouts to nigerian banks

you pick a bank and an account number, and seedless shows the account name before you confirm. you see the fee before you pay, every time: 0.5% on a payout, see fees.

stack

  • chain: solana mainnet
  • app: react native and expo, typescript, ios and android
  • api: typescript on the edge
  • on-chain and tooling: rust and typescript, open source at github.com/seedless-labs

security

seedless was audited by valves security in october 2026, and every finding has been fixed. to report a problem privately, read the security policy.
