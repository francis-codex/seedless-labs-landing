company

earn in dollars. spend at home.

seedless is a money app for people who get paid in dollars: freelancers, remote workers, designers, developers and creators. a client pays you in dollars, the dollars stay dollars in your account, and when you need naira you pay a nigerian bank in a few taps.

the problem

getting paid from abroad is easy now. turning those dollars into money you can spend at home still means a p2p trade with a stranger, a hidden spread, or a wait. most money apps that touch crypto also ask you to guard a seed phrase.

what seedless does

  • sign in with your face. no seed phrase, no password
  • hold dollars, receive and send for free. we cover the network fees
  • pay a nigerian bank, see the account name before you confirm
  • one price: 0.5% on a payout or a swap, shown before you confirm
  • non-custodial: your passkey signs, we never hold your funds

where it is

  • live on the app store, google play and the solana dapp store since 24 september 2026
  • version 1.0.6 live on 4 november 2026
  • as of 18 november 2026: 183 payouts to nigerian banks, $2,161.26 paid out, across 24 accounts, 13 of them repeat users

how we make money

0.5% on payouts and swaps. holding, receiving and sending are free. see fees.
